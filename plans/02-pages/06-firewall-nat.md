# Firewall & NAT (§5.6; PDF p. 18)

Join nftables counters to VyOS rules by comment/handle. Show chains, rules with hits, history, hide 0-hit toggle, and **"never hit in 30 d" dead-rule filter**, zone matrix, groups, NAT/NAT66, translations, conntrack statistics, flowtables (`[OFFLOAD]`/`[HW_OFFLOAD]` bits) and firewall logs. Counters refresh at 5 s, flowtables at 10 s; history is capped at 120 rules. Translations are an on-demand virtual table. Full table: [extraction](../brief-extracted.txt), page 18.
