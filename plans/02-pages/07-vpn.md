# VPN (§5.7; PDF p. 19)

WireGuard interfaces and peers via `wgctrl` every 5 s reconciled with ASK2 fast-path RX counters (`wgfp_rx`), handshake age and rx/tx history; show only public keys and never read private keys. Expected-traffic peer stale thresholds: warn >3 min, critical >15 min. IPsec read-only VICI/`swanctl` + `NETLINK_XFRM` / `/proc/net/xfrm_stat` (replay/auth errors) + CAAM crypto job counters (verifying HW offload vs SW fallback) every 10 s; OpenVPN status every 30 s; optional Tailscale/ZeroTier every 60 s and hidden when absent.
